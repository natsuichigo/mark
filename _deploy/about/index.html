Who I am

I work on the automation underneath infrastructure: the image pipelines, configuration management and CI/CD that let a team build a server or a platform the same way every time.

I have more than 10 years in enterprise IT, most of it in regulated financial services. Until September 2026 I was a Systems Engineer II and the Ansible Automation Platform Tech Lead at Capitec Bank, working on automation, server and storage engineering, observability and virtualisation across AWS and on-prem VMware.

I build CI/CD pipelines and Ansible-driven workflows, mentor other engineers, and I'm moving further into DevOps and platform engineering.
